Dot Sim

An unofficial Kode Dot simulator in your browser. Play an app, or open its code, change it and run it right here.

Pick an app on the Dot.

    

      Keys: ←↑→↓ d-pad,
      Z TOP (blue), X BOT (red). Click or tap the screen to touch.
      On the home screen, TOP or a tap opens an app; swipe or ←/→ for more.
      Hold TOP and BOT for a second (or Esc) to go back home.
    

    
Dot Sim is an unofficial simulator for the Kode Dot. It is not made, approved or supported by Kode. Kode, Kode Dot, kodeOS and Kody are trademarks of Kode.
